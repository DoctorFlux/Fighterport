## Reference and behavior

Retargeting, fitting and editing retain a full-quality editable target master.
Ordinary Save preserves that master. Explicit Exact / Error-bounded export copies
share the frozen-member preparation, verification and publication route. Search
effort does not change selected error limits. Unsupported absolute constraints
select Exact/reference output with an explanation.

The bounded route also declines unqualified native flag semantics: mismatched
animation/model compensation flags, Use-model S/R/T flags combined with authored
keys, or non-unit scale sharing a hierarchy path with compensation. The native
viewport does not independently implement every runtime flag policy. Exact
curve/flag preservation remains available; matching eligibility is not game
acceptance.

Compression measurements must compare the same finished target master with its
independent export candidate, using freshly written physical bytes. The original
Sonic donor is another animation on another skeleton and is not the compression
baseline. No fixed compression ratio or global size optimum is claimed.

## Why the master bake changed

An independently derived five-point polynomial certificate shows that a native
integer-only cubic cannot represent one authentic solved knee interval within
the required orientation budget. The source evidence is in
`probe-forward-feasibility40-polar1/knee-cubic-infeasibility.json`. Adding actual
legal fractional knots addresses the representation limit instead of relaxing
quality or trading large parent-joint errors for a precise end effector.

The original Next pose/IK algorithm remains. Its FromTo numerical helper now
retains small corrections rather than switching abruptly to identity. Generated
body Euler continuation follows a consistent trajectory around a near pole;
equivalent generated integer coordinates may change while physical integer
poses remain at native roundoff. Source-owned controls and imported authored
curves do not undergo this generated-chart rebranching. The dedicated 720°
fixture verifies that continuation does not reduce the intended turn modulo 360.
