# Format support and evidence

## 24 October 2026 audit baseline

The audited published baseline is Next `main`
`07cb03299b8a3e63f30642dd8e45a3403dacaaa7`, preview.14
(`2.0.0-preview.14-contact-authoring`). This documentation/source audit does not
record a new build, suite run, capture or physical-input test. The existing
[preview.14 acceptance record](CONTACT_AUTHORING_ACCEPTANCE_2026-10-24.md) reports
**373 native and 282 focused engine assertions (655)** and **47 actually inspected
PNG captures**. Those bounded receipts do not establish a full-suite pass or M2
completion; the retained advanced contact/release failure remains unresolved.

Source interpretation, motion quality, native serialization, and destination-tool
or game acceptance are separate evidence levels. Successful CHR0/project reopen
establishes the tested serialization behavior; image inspection and physical
pointer/DPI acceptance are also distinct. No Modkit import, target build or
in-game result follows from a saved path, native output or passing focused check.

Motion quality retains four independent gates: **action identity/style**,
**clearance/body volume**, **timing/continuity**, and **contacts**. None is promoted
to general acceptance by these focused authoring tests. Reviewed direct-chain
bone-point placement/releases are available; coupled surfaces, moving support
frames and automatic state releases remain open. Original Next v7 remains the
default retargeter; advanced 96.2 Apply stays blocked. Canonical per-build runtime
slot rules/Fill Missing, advanced companion portability/layers, remaining workspace
tools, physical input/DPI, generalization, non-Brawl adapters and games are queued
in the sole current M2 plan.

The Brawl format is not a required intermediate for future adapters. Unsupported
formats fail explicitly. No current claim of a playable fighter or target game test.

The current solver revision's complete-motion review uses one idle plus quick
and slow cliff get-up/attack in both directions. Running, takeoff, jumping,
falling and landing are also regression scopes. Seated and lying receipts are
from earlier milestone revisions. See `RETARGETING.md` for the exact scope,
native trajectory/contact bounds and executable hashes.

Future Xenoverse acceptance must include adult-to-child and child-to-adult body
proportions, source-skeleton-bound motion, and retargeting onto a supported Smash
target with its native writer. Goku/Gotenks are useful future real examples, not
special profiles or identity-dependent solver rules. No Xenoverse model or motion
has been loaded or exported in this milestone.

The supplied acceptance files exercise PAC model/motion containers and native
PAC and CHR0 output with fresh decoding. The disc-source case also inventories
real PCS costumes; standalone BRRES/MDL0 and native PCS writing have not received
separate output acceptance. Modern-game native writers are not present.

Library preview/reuse currently evaluates CHR0 skeletal channels. Other inherited
Brawl animation channels are inventoried with their exact native identities and
an unsupported status in this workflow. Original native resource editors remain
available. Unbound auxiliary skeletal tracks are reported and retained in source
metadata; their presence does not become a claim of complete accessory export.
Automatic associations require a reviewed pair, or reviewed native Brawl build
ownership plus a unique original body candidate and native body-track binding.
Ambiguous assets remain unassociated. No reusable automatic rig-profile store,
Melee HSD, Ultimate SSBH, Unreal or Xenoverse native adapter is implemented here.
