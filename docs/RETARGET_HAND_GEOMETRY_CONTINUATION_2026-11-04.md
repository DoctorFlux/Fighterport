## Demonstrated finger-axis defect and narrow code change

In the pinned Sonic `AttackS3S` → Mario target, the thumb (`finger4`) is the main digit-level mismatch. At frame 7 its source left hand-local skin-centroid motion is approximately `(0.437, 0.714, 0.097)` while the original target thumb moves `(0.048, -0.568, -0.724)`. The other four matched digits have much smaller centroid errors. Both digits are mapped; the discrepancy occurs before CHR0 writing. The source and target proximal finger segments point in different bind directions, but the existing world-bind rotation transfer used the same hand-frame rotation axis.

`HumanoidRetargeter` now aligns each mapped proximal digit's measured bind segment axis in its parent frame before carrying the source-authored rotation. It requires actual paired proximal/distal joints and matching mapped direct parents, leaves bind transforms and skin weights intact, and reduces to the old authored pose when axes match. The rule is anatomical, with no character/action-specific angle. A procedural unequal-axis, same-axis and unchanged-bind regression adds three checks to the coordinate-frame suite: **97/97 pass**.

The full 44-frame result serialized and freshly reopened: **284,268 bytes**, versus **283,752** for the preceding native result. Exact source-owned root locals and the existing native trajectory/contact checks still pass. The early palm clearance discrepancy improves from **0.432063** to **0.374697** arm lengths, but its bound remains **0.05**. Forearm discrepancy remains **0.261322** versus **0.10**; extra trunk descent remains **1.949278** native units versus **0.039408**. The hand fix is **not complete**. Existing authentic `DownStandU` and `LandingAirN` contact suites pass **25/25** and **27/27**, and procedural terminal contacts pass **116/116** after the digit change. No new visual, human-input or game-runtime claim was made.

## Isolated geometry trials, all unaccepted

The lowest whole-hand point is not a stable wrist goal. At frame 7 the source lowest left point lies on a fourth finger, while the target's lowest point lies on its thumb. The target thumb-only skin minimum moves from **−2.405** to **−1.28** under anatomical axis transfer, much closer to its source thumb minimum **−1.13**. Early frames 5–6 still have a target **palm** minimum roughly one unit lower than the source palm. Therefore neither the earlier lowest-point goal nor a thumb-only correction fixes the palm.

A read-only frame-7 body/arm search showed why a hand-height-only solution is misleading. Raising the target pelvis skin to its source-relative trunk clearance leaves the old wrist goal **1.39** native units beyond the available posed arm reach. Translating the target chest by about `(0, −1, −2)` can make the sampled hand/forearm/trunk *height* numbers pass, but moves palm/forearm centroids **0.87–1.11 arm lengths** from the source-relative 3D goals. A rotated-spine/chest candidate fails the height gates. An 8% left upper-arm scale reaches sampled height bounds but leaves **0.53–1.05 arm-length** 3D region errors. These are unacceptable shape/motion changes, not a correction. The source and target pelvis world rotations at frame 7 are effectively the same; their lowest torso skin points have different weights and morphology. Even a ±90° two-axis spine pose search raises the target torso skin only to **0.694** against the source-relative **1.878** target. No bind, weight or target-only `WaistNb` was changed.

A separate hand-only read-only search varied wrist height and elbow pole around the unchanged target body.
