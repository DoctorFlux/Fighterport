## Hand-contact counterexample and quality gate

The 49-quarter-frame read-only early witness (native time 0–12) found **zero** planted-hand proposals and **zero** full-support hand samples. At frames 8, 10 and 12 its `GroundEvidence` is zero. The source left/right hand-patch signed heights are approximately `(-0.89, -0.63)`, `(-0.69, -0.47)` and `(-0.75, -0.50)` native units. Before body correction, the target hand patches are roughly `-1.7` to `-1.82`; after the body candidate they hover at roughly `+0.12` to `+0.25` at those samples. A raised body therefore removes extra target penetration but does **not** establish hand-ground agreement. No source hand pin, floor contact, or lock may be inferred from low height alone in a non-grounded attack interval.

The restored production baseline confirms the discrepancy **precedes native writing**. At frame 5 source L/R palms are `-0.330/-0.461` versus raw/reopened Mario `-1.429/-1.553`; at frame 8 source `-0.886/-0.628` versus Mario `-1.820/-1.704`; at frame 11 source `-0.738/-0.508` versus Mario `-1.812/-1.722`, all in native units. Raw, solved and freshly reopened palm heights agree. The maximum normalized palm difference over frames 5–12 is `0.4320626` of arm length, forearm difference `0.2613219`, and extra source-relative aggregate trunk/body-surface descent `1.9493` target native units. The solver proposed **no** hand or forearm support/lock. Sonic's palms are flat but moving quickly at frame 5; by frames 10–12 they are flat and slow. This is a near-ground geometric witness, not an approved planted-contact timeline.

The body-only candidate failed broad retarget quality. Its residual target whole-mesh depth differed from the source and the hand patches disagreed in sign and height. The current contact proposal has no early hand support, so the existing supported-contact pass does not cover this discrepancy. A general correction needs source-relative hand/body geometry goals or an explicit reviewed contact context, with unchanged frozen support gates and no character/action numerical route. Do not accept the clip based on its native reopen, smaller penetration number, or a visual floor guide.

An initial signed-hand-depth candidate passed its procedural cases but **failed the fresh authentic rerun**. The v2 native result reopens at 302,376 bytes (SHA-256 `bed91d7748b7945f111fb006e049567f47b6d9fd538f6c02a240e30a614fbec8`), retains exact root-local motion and passes 474 trajectory/345 supported-contact samples, but the independent early-hand gate reports maximum normalized clearance error `0.3661521345` versus its frozen `0.05` per-arm-length bound. That is essentially unchanged from the body-only baseline `0.366152132`. The observed early hand heights remain above the target guide at frames 8/10/12. The initial signed-depth window excluded the source palms' deeper negative range, so its synthetic pass was insufficient evidence for this authentic case. The failed candidate and its ignored `Fighterport-hand-depth-v2-authentic` receipt were retained; no support was invented and no bound was relaxed.

A widened signed-depth window still failed the authentic v4 case with the same output SHA and `0.3661521345` normalized error. The independent reach witness establishes a **geometric conflict** for a hand-only IK fix after the aggregate trunk/body lift: at native frame 5 the left/right shoulder-to-desired-palm distances are `5.05/4.87` against reachable chain lengths `4.54/4.47`; at frame 7.25 they are `6.40/5.97` against `5.50/5.34`. At frame 10 the left side is `4.55 > 4.34` while the right side is `4.17 < 4.26`. These finite samples explain why changing a hand-height window alone cannot repair the real clip. They do not prove the full joint pose is infeasible when the trunk, shoulders and pelvis are solved together. The supported-contact solver already has coupled body controls, but it currently admits only reviewed contact goals; a source-relative passing-hand height would need a separate noncontact residual and conflict checks, not a fabricated hand support timeline.

Their exact private interval and failure receipts are under ignored `artifacts/Fighterport-coupled-v1-*` through `Fighterport-coupled-v9b-*`. All experimental solver code was removed.

A later read-only v6 witness identifies Sonic's upper-arm parent as its chest joint, while Mario has extra L/R shoulder parents between chest and upper arms. The target-only parents are unmapped and remain at their bind-local transforms. The later experimental trials kept source roots and target bind/skin data unchanged. A body lift plus arm goals serialized and reopened, but its maximum early palm/forearm errors were `0.240892/0.348090` arm lengths, over both frozen bounds. Rotating the target-only unmapped shoulder parent failed native fractional preparation at bone 24, frames 2–3, with `19.567266°` rotation error. An internally tighter bake passed the same final `0.0002` gate but did not fix the combined motion. These results are in ignored `artifacts/Fighterport-temporal-trial-authentic*` receipts; none qualified a product correction.

Arm rotations alone reopened and retained root/support checks. They improved early forearm error to `0.072672 < 0.10` but left palm error `0.151589 > 0.05` and aggregate trunk/body-surface extra descent `1.949278` target units. A later isolated mapped-arm trial reached palm `0.047087 < 0.05` and forearm `0.084355 < 0.10` across the frozen early samples and reopened a `353,340`-byte CHR0 (SHA-256 `2fc9594003df4beaeeb95a08b41e87a7f417b5461efcda347bf120aca2477a3b`). Its trunk extra descent remained `1.949278` target units, and the worst target `HatN` vertex remained `-3.79684` against the bind-plane guide. The result therefore **fails the combined body/hand objective** despite the hand-only pass. At frame 6.5 the right hand-patch normal was already closely aligned with the source (`0.9954/0.9975`), flatness was `1`, and no wrist twist was needed for that sample.

The read-only v16 whole-skin diagnostic compared this candidate with the same target baseline at 49 quarter-frame times over all 3,913 vertices. The maximum vertex shift was `2.810823` native units at frame 12; vertices near the shoulder/arm shifted by up to `1.322490`. For 32 nearest bind shoulder/torso pairs per side, maximum gap changes were `0.682011` left and `1.182349` right. Repeated and reverse-order pose evaluation was bitwise deterministic, but no reviewed shoulder-seam acceptance bound exists. These finite measurements rule out treating the hand-only pass as a full-skin quality pass. The ignored receipt is `artifacts/Fighterport-temporal-trial-authentic16/reviewed-cranial-retarget.json`; it does not establish continuous-time or game behavior. The experimental production hooks and diagnostic trial modes were reverted.

The next diagnostic should compare source and target arm-chain world transforms and skinned support across bind and animated frames before assigning causality. The next implementation needs a deterministic offline **time-continuous** coupled body/shoulder/arm trajectory. It must keep passing hands as noncontact goals, preserve source-relative palm and forearm motion and skinned trunk depth, and retain exact roots and established supports. A per-time reach solution that passes a static pose but cannot be represented through native fractional samples is insufficient. The frozen authentic proposal bounds were palm clearance `≤0.05` and forearm clearance `≤0.10` of target arm length over frames `5..12`, with target trunk extra descent `≤0.01` target leg length; no candidate met the full pipeline. These are candidate acceptance checks, not a claim that the original animation passed them.
